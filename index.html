BlunnyLogger

A reading tracker for what you've read, what you're reading and what's next.

Getting help

Email 7chimi.dev@gmail.com with what you were doing and what went wrong, and a screenshot if you can. The version number is at the bottom of the Settings tab.

Common questions

How do I move my library to a new phone?
On the old phone, open Settings → Export Backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (Files, iCloud Drive, email). On the new phone, open Settings → Import from File and pick it. Covers download again by themselves.
Why is a book missing its cover?
Covers come from Open Library, which doesn't have every edition. Tap the book's cover to search again, or choose a photo instead.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything is stored on your phone; only finding and downloading covers needs a connection.
