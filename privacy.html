BlunnyLogger Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

In short: BlunnyLogger keeps your reading library on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer never receives your library or anything about how you use the app.

BlunnyLogger is a personal reading tracker for iPhone and Android, made by David Hazama ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — books, series, authors, dates, ratings, tags, notes, loans and your settings — is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Cover images are saved there too. None of it is sent to me or to any server I run; I don't run any.

What the app sends over the internet

The only online service BlunnyLogger talks to is Open Library, a free book catalogue run by the non-profit Internet Archive. It is used for cover art:

  • Finding a cover: when you tap Find Cover Art (or run it for your whole library), the app sends the book's title and author to Open Library's search.
  • Downloading a cover: the chosen image is downloaded from Open Library's cover service and saved on your device, so your library then works offline.

These requests contain only the title and author (or the cover's catalogue number), plus a label identifying the app and a contact address for the developer, which Open Library asks apps to send. They contain nothing that identifies you. Like any website, Open Library can see the IP address the request comes from; its handling of that is covered by the Internet Archive's own privacy policy.

Photos

If you choose a cover from your photos, the app uses the system photo picker: you pick one image, and only that image is copied into the app. BlunnyLogger does not ask for, and does not have, access to the rest of your photo library.

Backups and files you export

  • Export Backup writes your library to a file in a place you choose. What happens to that file afterwards — sharing it, storing it in a cloud drive — is up to you.
  • Import from File reads a file you pick and adds its books to the library on your device.
  • Your phone's own backup (iCloud or computer backup on iPhone; Google backup on Android) may include the app's library, as it does for most apps. That is managed by Apple or Google under your account, not by me. Cover images are left out of these backups, since they can be downloaded again.

What BlunnyLogger does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.

Apple may give me anonymous, aggregate statistics such as download counts and, if you've chosen to share them with developers in your iPhone's settings, crash reports. These come from Apple and don't include your library.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device. Copies in files you exported, or in your phone's backups, are removed through the Files app or your Apple or Google account.

Children

BlunnyLogger isn't directed at children, and since it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed before the new version is released.
